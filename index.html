The fine print

Good questions.

Is this legal advice?+

No. OpenLegalData is research infrastructure. Results should be reviewed by a qualified professional and checked against official sources before they are relied on.

How complete is the coverage?+

Coverage varies by source. Some collections are broad, while others are representative subsets. The skills surface these limitations and point you to official sources when needed.

Why separate skills instead of one legal search?+

Case discovery, citation verification, clause research, and statutory lookup are different jobs. Separate skills preserve the right search strategy and guardrails for each one.

Will it stay free?+

The skill code is Apache 2.0 licensed. Live API access and future team capabilities may evolve as the service grows; any paid offering will be explained clearly before it applies.
